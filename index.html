01 — A letter

Dear friends and family,

Our first date was an Ed Sheeran concert. You might say everything about it, and everything since, has been "Perfect."

Ashley joined run club for the running. Charles joined for Ashley. She's much faster, so he's been chasing her ever since. Along the way we fostered fourteen cats. Twelve found wonderful homes. Two took one look at us and decided they already had.

Your only job is to show up in linen. It's going to be perfect. We checked.

With all our love,

Bali runs on Central Indonesia Time, fifteen hours ahead of Los Angeles. When we say our vows at 4:30 on Saturday afternoon, it will be 1:30 on Saturday morning back home. Set your watch on the plane, not after.

03 — Order of events

A weekend on the cliff

Everything happens at AYANA Bali in Jimbaran, on the estate where you're staying.

  1. Friday

    27 August · Welcome
    • 6:00 PM
      Welcome party at Rock Bar

      Drinks and bites on the rocks at the foot of the cliff, with the Indian Ocean breaking underneath. Take the inclinator down. Come as you are off the plane.

  2. Saturday

    28 August · The celebration
    • 4:00 PM
      Guests seated

      The clifftop lawn above the ocean. There's shade, and there's water.

    • 4:30 PM
      Ceremony

      Short and sweet, then straight to the drinks.

    • 5:15 PM
      Sunset drinks

      Cocktails at the cliff edge while the sun goes into the Indian Ocean.

    • 7:00 PM
      Reception

      Dinner, speeches kept mercifully brief, and dancing until the estate asks us to stop.

  3. Sunday

    29 August · Yours
    • All day
      Nothing planned

      We're not hosting anything on Sunday. Sleep in, find a pool, and fly home whenever you're ready.

Every event takes place on the AYANA estate, and you'll be staying on it too. Nobody drives anywhere all weekend. The resort runs a shuttle between AYANA Resort, AYANA Villas and RIMBA around the clock.

Getting there

Fly from Los Angeles (LAX) to Denpasar (DPS). There's no nonstop, so you'll connect once. AYANA is 25 minutes from the airport, and we'll have cars waiting when you land.

Staying

Rooms are held for our guests across AYANA Resort, AYANA Villas and RIMBA, all on the same estate. We'll send booking details once you've replied.

04 — Dress code

Tropical formal

Linen suits, long dresses, real color. Dress for a beautiful evening outdoors in the tropics, not a ballroom.

  • Palm
  • Sage
  • Ocean
  • Clay
  • Husk
  • Sand
  • Andesite
  • Not ivory

Fabric

It will be around 29°C and humid at half past four. Linen, cotton voile, silk and seersucker will carry you through. Wool, polyester and anything lined will not.

Shoes

The ceremony is on clifftop stone and Rock Bar is built on the rocks at the water's edge. Block heels, flat sandals and loafers survive both. Stilettos lose on the first night.

05 — A note on children

Adults only

We love your children. We're also asking you to leave them at home this time.

The weekend runs late, the cliffs are real, and the bar never quite closes. We would rather you had a few days that felt like a holiday of your own. We've kept the guest list to adults, and we hope you'll take that as an invitation to sleep in.
