    05 — Tea ceremony

    The tea ceremony

    Rooted in centuries of Chinese tradition, the tea ceremony is a symbolic way of honoring our elders, expressing gratitude, and welcoming the joining of two families.

    Before the ceremony, Charles will take on the playful tradition of door games, proving his love and earning his way to Ashley. Together, we will then serve tea to our elders and receive their blessings as we begin our marriage.

    For us, it is a meaningful way to honor Ashley’s Chinese heritage while celebrating the coming together of our Chinese and Japanese families.

    07 — Questions

    You might be wondering

    Here are a few details to help
    you plan your trip to Bali.

    1. When should I fly in?

      Into Denpasar (DPS), by Friday afternoon for the welcome party and tea ceremony at Rock Bar. It’s a long trip with a connection, so build in the time.

    2. Do I need a visa?

      US passports get a 30-day visa on arrival, plus a small tourist levy. Six months’ validity and a blank page. We’ll send the current rules in the spring.

    3. Where do I stay?

      On the estate. Rooms are held across AYANA Resort, AYANA Villas and RIMBA. Booking details follow your reply.

    4. What’s the weather like?

      Dry season, and the best of it. Around 84°F in the afternoon, 75°F at night, humid, and very little rain. The sun sets a little after 6 PM.

    5. What about money?

      Indonesia uses the rupiah. The resort takes cards for everything. A little cash is handy for taxis, markets and tips, and the airport ATMs are the easiest place to get it.

    6. Do I need a car?

      No. Everything is on the estate and the shuttle runs around the clock. Gojek, Grab or a taxi from the airport also work well.

    7. Are children invited?

      We love your little ones, but our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a chance to enjoy a relaxing getaway.

    8. When do I need to reply by?

      Please RSVP by January 31, 2027. This helps us with final planning and room blocks.
